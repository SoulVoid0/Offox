# Welcome to {{SITE_TITLE}}

**{{SITE_TITLE}}** is your own encyclopedia. It lives in a folder on your computer or external drive, so it works without the internet and nobody else can change it.

## Getting started

- Read [[Editing help]] to learn how to write articles.
- Use **Create a page** in the sidebar, or search for a title that does not exist yet and follow the red link.
- Upload pictures with **Upload file**, or with the 🖼 button while editing.
- See every article on the [list of all pages](special.html?p=all).

## Did you know…

- Every article is a plain Markdown file in the `articles` folder, so you can also edit them with any text editor.
- Older versions of each article are kept in the `history` folder.
- FireLine, the Offox search engine, searches this wiki together with all your other websites.
